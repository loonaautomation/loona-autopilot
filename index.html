Webhook — the doorway

The moment a customer sends a message, the webhook carries it straight into the system. Not a second of delay.

Gemini AI — the brain

Google's AI reads the message: what the customer wants, which language they are speaking, and how serious they are.

Google Sheets — the register

Every lead is logged automatically — name, service, budget, score. Nothing ever gets lost.

Gmail / Alerts — the news

The reply to the customer and the alert to the owner go out on their own. When a Hot lead arrives, the owner knows instantly.
